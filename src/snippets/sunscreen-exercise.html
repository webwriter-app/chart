Exploring Monthly Sales Data

You work as a data analyst for SunscreenFans & Co GmbH, a company that sells sunscreen and fans across Europe. You have a full year of monthly data featuring three metrics: Income (€m), Revenue (€m), and Units Sold. Use the Charts Widget to complete the following tasks:

1. Choose the best chart type

Explore the given dataset and modify your widget by adding and renaming three new charts:

  • Chart 1 ("Revenue vs. Income"): Select the chart type best suited for comparing distinct, side-by-side values of revenue and income for each individual month.

  • Chart 2 ("Income Trend"): Select the chart type best suited for visualizing the continuous, chronological trend of income over the year. Filter for the value specifically.

  • Chart 3 ("Revenue Trend"): Select the chart type best suited for visualizing the continuous, chronological trend of revenue over the year. Filter for the value specifically.

2. Comparing the trends

Open your newly created Income Trend and Revenue Trend tabs and analyze the full year (January to December).

  • Describe in your own words how both datasets change as the year progresses.

  • In which months is the income the highest and in which is the revenue the highest?

  • Do the two trend lines share a similar shape? Based on the nature of the company's products, what does this tell you about sales?

3. Working with the Scatterplot

Switch to a Scatter Plot tab. Plot Units Sold on the x-axis and Revenue on the y-axis.

  • Observe the distribution of the data points. Which months represent the highest and lowest sales performance?

  • Select from the regression models the linear regression and add it to the plot. Which monthly data points sit significantly above or below this trendline?

  • What real-world business factors might cause a month's revenue to deviate from the typical trendline of units sold?
